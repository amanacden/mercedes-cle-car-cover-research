## Non-negotiable buying criteria
- Car: 2024 Mercedes-Benz **CLE 300 4MATIC Cabriolet A236**, black exterior, fabric top, used outdoors in Jersey City, NJ.
- Correct model geometry: Cabriolet, not C236 Coupe; confirm mirror pockets and 20-inch AMG wheel/bumper configuration only as relevant to hem clearance. Verify dimensions/trim with seller.
- Suitable for rain, snow, UV, wind, dust, soft-top fabric and black paint; avoid abrasive inner surface and trapped moisture.
- **Return preference: full refund after at least 15 days of installed outdoor use, even if simply dissatisfied**, with no restocking, shipping or repackaging fees. Ask whether original tax and shipping are refunded too.
- Optimize *total dollars at risk*, not only sticker price.

## Parallel kickoff tasks
- **Track A — fit:** merchant confirms A236 Cabriolet, mirror pockets and no harmful soft-top/paint contact.
- **Track B — refund:** merchant confirms exact use allowance, full refund after 15+ days, prepaid label, no deductions, time starts on delivery.
- **Track C — materials:** compare inner lining, permeability/breathability, seam leakage, tie-down performance and ease of drying/cleaning.
- **Track D — economics:** obtain actual checkout total, delivery ETA, expected lifespan, return risk and competitor pricing.
- **Track E — parking:** confirm intended street/driveway setup and whether cover can stay secured.

## Sequential hard gates
1. Gate 0: Correct vehicle + actual parking exposure known.
2. Gate 1: Candidate clearly fits **A236 Cabriolet**; reject Coupe-only and ambiguous "CLE-Class" without confirmation.
3. Gate 2: Seller agrees to **used 15+ days / full refund**, unless owner explicitly waives it.
4. Gate 3: Roof/paint protection and wind behavior acceptable, supported by product materials/instructions.
5. Gate 4: Compare **delivered** price and exposure to lost shipping, depreciation, restock, theft and extra maintenance.
6. Gate 5: Order, log deadlines, inspect before installation; return promptly if defective.
7. Gate 6: During permitted test period check for rubbing, moisture retention and poor fastening; keep or initiate refund.
