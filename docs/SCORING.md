# Scoring rubric and financial decision model

## Gate first, score second
A high rating does NOT offset a failed hard gate. Reject or put **HOLD** if:
- Not explicitly compatible with **2024 CLE 300 Cabriolet A236**.
- Unsupported for outdoor soft-top use or unacceptable trapped moisture/wind abrasion risk.
- No written merchant promise of a full refund after 15+ days of **actual outdoor use**, unless owner records a deliberate exception.
- Physical parking rules render cover impractical.

## Weighted score (0–100; only for candidates that cleared gates)

| Factor | Weight | Full marks require |
|---|---:|---|
| Return certainty after 15+ days of real use | 30 | Binding written seller confirmation: full price + tax + original shipping + prepaid return, zero deductions |
| Convertible-specific fit & top/paint compatibility | 25 | A236 pattern, safe mirror/roof coverage, soft nonabrasive inner surface, confirmed dimensions |
| Weather protection / breathability / wind stability | 20 | Rain/snow and UV suitable, ventilation, well-secured straps, reliable workmanship |
| Net cost / expected ownership cost | 15 | Lower landed price with no hidden fees or excessive longevity tradeoff |
| Delivery speed and dependable seller support | 10 | Clear ETA, credible support, useful warranty |

**Status:** all current candidates are **unapproved** against the strict return rule; do not assign them 90+ just for marketing claims.

## Total cost formulas
- **Landed price** = advertised price + sales tax + outbound shipping + accessories + import/duties (if any) − valid discounts.
- **Refund shortfall** = landed price − actual refund after return − any costs already included in landed price but not refundable (check calculation to avoid double count).
- **Expected acquisition cost** = (probability kept × landed price) + (probability returned × refund shortfall) + attributable maintenance/storage time cost.
- **Expected two-year cost** = landed price + straps/cleaning/replacements + risk-weighted paint/roof damage exposure − expected resale value of the cover.
- **Decision alternative:** compare cost of better parking or no cover with the cover's maintenance, purchase cost and incremental abrasion risk. Do not assert a paint-damage probability without evidence.

## Example, illustrative only
A $230 cover with a **20% restock charge** exposes **$46 plus return shipping and any nonrefunded tax/delivery** on a normal change-of-mind return. A $75 cover with the same fee exposes $15 plus shipping. Price should not be compared without the relevant return terms.

## Inspection protocol after ordering
1. On arrival, compare SKU against A236 Cabriolet and photograph packaging, lining, seams and straps.
2. On a **clean, dry, cool car**, and only where permitted, check initial fit (no sharp hems, mirror tension or contact with movable trim).
3. Avoid use during strong wind until anchoring is proven; do not allow dirt between cover and paint.
4. Monitor rain aftermath for trapped moisture; remove and dry if needed. No cover is a substitute for a maintained soft top.
5. If testing is allowed by seller, record fit and photos within the exact confirmed return window.
6. Initiate a written return before the earliest deadline if unsatisfied; save carrier scan and check actual refund.
