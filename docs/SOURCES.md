## Research hygiene
- Listings, prices, stock, seller identity and terms may change. Snapshot date ≠ purchase-day verification.
- Do not confuse a warranty for manufacturing defects with a change-of-mind return right.
- Do not confuse "30-day returns" with "30 days of installed outdoor use."
- Use direct written confirmation for the exact SKU; archive screenshots or PDFs **locally**, not with personal data in this public repo.
