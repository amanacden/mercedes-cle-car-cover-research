## Vehicle/constraints
- Model: **2024 CLE 300 4MATIC Cabriolet**, A236 platform, fabric soft top; **not CLE Coupe C236** or C-Class Cabriolet.
- Black exterior, New Jersey outdoor exposure. Assume both street and driveway parking are possible; verify actual intended spot.
- Mercedes-Benz quoted dimensions for CLE Cabriolet: **4,850 × 1,861 × 1,424 mm** (L × W × H), excluding any unlisted protrusions.
- Cover must work with the **soft top fully closed**, mirrors, and wind-deflector in the stowed position. Ask seller to confirm fit for VIN/trim before ordering; do not publish VIN in this public repo.
- A nominal product rating or warranty is not evidence of full refunds after use.
- **Never mark a product 'approved' until written seller confirmation AND actual listing/order policy evidence are saved.**
